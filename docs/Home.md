# Dashboard

One page that lists every capstone and portfolio service with a live status dot, the stack, an Open link and its brief. Portfolio, front door, by Maximus Friedrich Baluyot.

## Pages
- [Getting started](Getting-Started.md): run it in three commands, what you see, the API.
- [How it was built](How-It-Was-Built.md): from the brief to a running service, step by step.
- [Results](Results.md): the numbers and what they mean.
- [Limits and next steps](Limits-and-Next-Steps.md): what it cannot do yet.

## The brief
Fourteen services on fourteen ports are hard to show in an interview. The dashboard is the one address to open: it groups the five capstone services and the eight portfolio apps by target role, shows which are up right now, and opens each one or its brief in a click. "Solved" is an interviewer finding a working demo within ten seconds of opening http://localhost:5173/.
